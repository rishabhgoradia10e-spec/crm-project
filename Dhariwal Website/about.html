Dhariwal Securities was founded on a simple philosophy: capital preservation is the prerequisite to capital growth. For over three decades, we have guided corporate treasuries, family offices, and individual builders through complex financial cycles.

Headquartered in Mumbai, we maintain direct trading memberships with the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), and operate as an registered Depository Participant with CDSL.

Our Journey

Milestones of Stability & Trust

1991

Establishment & Foundations

Founded by Rameshwar Dhariwal as an equity broking house in Mumbai to serve private business owners navigating the post-liberalization market boom.

2002

Direct Corporate memberships

Secured trading seats at BSE and NSE, upgrading technical terminals to process high-volume corporate treasury executions.

2012

Wealth Advisory Expansion

Inaugurated dedicated Private Wealth Management departments and established CDSL Depository Participant relationships to offer integrated accounts.

2020

Quantitative PMS Launch

Recruited Dr. Aditi Dhariwal to design and administer bespoke Portfolio Management Services (PMS), crossing the 15,000 Crore AUA milestone.
